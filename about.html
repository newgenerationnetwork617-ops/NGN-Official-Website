Our story

A generation raised to live for Christ.

New Generation Network exists to spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ, inspire lives, strengthen faith, and bring hope. Our heart is to make Christ-centred teaching and encouragement accessible to people everywhere.

Our mission

Sharing the Gospel. Strengthening faith. Bringing hope.

We use biblical teaching and uplifting Christian resources to encourage people to know Christ, grow in faith, and live out the truth of God's Word.

“Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.”
